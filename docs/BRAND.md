# Kairo Systems — Brand Identity

## Name

**Kairo Systems** (short form: **Kairo**).

From the Greek *kairos* — "the opportune moment". For a small business, that moment is the instant a customer gets in touch. The whole proposition is about never missing it. "Systems" signals engineered, dependable infrastructure rather than a one-off freelancer or a gimmicky "AI" label.

> **Before you trade:** check availability with Companies House, the UK IPO trade mark register (classes 35, 42) and your preferred domains (e.g. `kairosystems.co.uk`, `kairo.systems`). If it's taken, the brand system works unchanged with an alternative such as *Kairon*, *Meridian Systems* or *Apsis Systems*.

## Positioning

**For** UK owner-managed businesses that win work by responding quickly (trades, agents, clinics, recruiters, gyms, hospitality, garages, professional services),
**Kairo** designs, tests and runs the AI systems that answer every enquiry, book the appointment and follow up automatically,
**unlike** freelancers and DIY tools, every Kairo workflow is tested, approved by the client before launch and monitored daily — with transparent monthly pricing.

## Messaging

- **Primary line:** *Every enquiry answered. Every opportunity captured.*
- **Supporting line:** *AI automation designed around your business, tested before launch and monitored every day.*
- **Proof pillars:** Tested before trusted · Approved knowledge only · Monitored daily · Secure by design
- **Value language:** speed-to-lead, recovered opportunities, hours saved, fewer no-shows. Avoid hype words ("revolutionary", "10x", "AI-powered everything").

### Voice
Confident, plain-spoken, British English, honest. We say what something does and what it costs. We never promise results we can't evidence and we label examples as examples.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "Answers your calls when the team can't." | "Revolutionise your business with cutting-edge AI!" |
| "Illustrative scenario — not a client result." | Invented testimonials or statistics |
| "Pricing depends on your volumes and integrations." | "Guaranteed ROI" |

## Logo concept

A rounded-square tile (ink background, hairline border) containing a stylised **K** built from two strokes:
1. a **vertical bar** — stability, the business;
2. a **forward chevron** — momentum, the response;
3. a **white signal node** where they meet — *the moment an opportunity is captured*.

Strokes use the Signal→Aura gradient. The wordmark sets **Kairo** in semibold and **Systems** in regular, muted. Source: `src/components/ui/logo.tsx` (pure SVG — export to PNG/SVG for print or social from the rendered component). Minimum size 20 px; keep clear space equal to the node diameter.

Ratio guide: ~85% ink, ~10% neutrals, ~5% Signal, Aura only inside gradients and occasional emphasis. All tokens live in `src/app/globals.css` (`@theme`).

## Typography

- **Geist Sans** (headlines tight tracking `-0.02em`, body 15–17 px, line-height 1.6)
- **Geist Mono** for eyebrows, data labels and the demo console — reinforces the "engineered systems" feel.
- Both are open-source (SIL OFL) and self-hosted via the `geist` package — no third-party font requests.

## Visual motifs

- Fine 56 px grid fading out from the top of hero sections
- Soft radial Signal/Aura glows behind hero content (never behind body text)
- Product UI as imagery: dashboards, workflow steps and data panels — **no stock robots, brains, or circuit-board clichés**
- Motion: short fade-up reveals and a single interactive workflow demo; respects `prefers-reduced-motion`

## Application examples

- Email signature: name, role, `Kairo Systems`, phone, website — no images required.
- Social avatar: the logo tile on Ink 950.
- Proposal documents: generated by the platform (`/proposal/[token]`) and print cleanly to PDF in light mode.
